# Benefits of building your store on Shopify

Shopify software development involves creating custom applications, themes, and plugins to enhance the functionality of the Shopify platform. This can be beneficial for businesses that want to create a unique online store that stands out from the competition.

One of the main benefits of Shopify is the ability to create custom themes. A custom theme can help your online store stand out and create a unique brand identity. A well-designed theme can also improve the user experience and help increase sales.

Another benefit of Shopify software development is the ability to create custom plugins. Plugins can be used to add functionality to your online store, such as advanced search capabilities, social media integration, or custom payment gateways. Custom plugins can be tailored to meet the specific needs of your business and help improve the overall performance of your online store.

# Shopify App Development

Shopify app development is another area of software development that can benefit your business. Apps are essentially plugins that can be installed on a Shopify store to enhance its functionality. Thousands of apps are available in the Shopify App Store, but sometimes businesses need something more specific to their needs.

Custom app development allows businesses to create a unique app tailored to their specific needs. For example, if you run a subscription-based business, you may need an app to manage subscriptions and recurring payments. A custom app can be developed to meet this specific need and integrated into your Shopify store.
