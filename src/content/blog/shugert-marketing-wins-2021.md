The entire Shugert Marketing team is absolutely excited to announce that we’ve made it on [Clutch’s list](https://clutch.co/mx/it-services/analytics "Clutch's list") of the top service providers!

That’s right! In addition to this sweet recognition, Shugert Marketing is also heading in the European market! We’re excited to open a new chapter to our journey and grow further!

Thank you so much to Clutch for this award. This honor is a representation of the amazing relationships we have with our clients. It is because of their remarkable reviews that we receive this honor; what more can we ask?
