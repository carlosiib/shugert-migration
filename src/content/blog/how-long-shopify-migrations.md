Migrating your website to Shopify is one of the smartest moves you can make if you’re serious about ecommerce growth. But let’s be honest — it can also feel overwhelming.

How long does a Shopify migration take? What does it actually involve? And is it worth switching from platforms like WordPress or Squarespace?

This guide breaks down the Shopify migration timeline, step-by-step process, and smart shortcuts to launch faster without headaches.

# What Is a Shopify Migration?

A **Shopify migration** is the process of moving your current website — including products, customers, orders, blog content, and design — to the Shopify platform.

Migrations typically include:

- Product and inventory data
- Customer profiles and purchase history
- Existing blog posts or landing pages
- Site design and structure
- URL redirects (for SEO)

# How Long Does a Shopify Migration Take?

**The short answer:**

> **Most Shopify migrations take 1–12 weeks depending on the size and complexity of your store.**

## Shopify Migration Timeframes (by Store Type)

| Store Type                                               | Approximate Migration Time |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| 🛍️ Basic store (under 100 SKUs)                          | 3–5 days                   |
| 🧰 Medium store (100–500 SKUs, basic CMS)                | 7–14 days                  |
| 🧩 Large or complex store (1,000+ SKUs, custom features) | 4+ weeks                   |

> 🧠 **Pro tip: If you’re migrating from WooCommerce, Magento, or Squarespace, your timeline may be shorter with a migration tool like LitExtension.**

## What Impacts Shopify Migration Time?

**Several factors determine how long your Shopify migration will take:**

| Factor                           | Impact on Time                                             |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🔢 **Number of products & SKUs** | More products = longer imports                             |
| 📐 **Custom themes & layout**    | Custom code or design adds complexity                      |
| 🤝 **3rd-party apps or plugins** | Reintegrations may require development time                |
| 🔄 **SEO redirects**             | Mapping old URLs to new ones is crucial but time-consuming |
| 👥 **Customer/order data**       | More history = more careful data transfer                  |

# ⚡ How to Speed Up a Shopify Migration

Want to launch faster? **Hire an Expert** or use these proven strategies to cut your migration time in half:

### 1. Start with a Prebuilt Shopify Theme

Skip the custom design initially. Pick a **well-optimized, mobile-friendly theme** from the Shopify Theme Store, then refine your design post-launch.

### 2. Clean and Prepare Data First

Before migration:

- Standardize product names, tags, and collections
- Compress and rename image files
- Remove outdated SKUs

### 3. Handle SEO Redirects Proactively

Use Shopify’s **URL redirect tool** or a migration plugin to 301 redirect all important pages from your old site to your new Shopify URLs.

> 🔍 **Redirects preserve search rankings and prevent 404 errors.**

### 4. Test in a Staging Environment

Before launching, test everything in a **duplicate theme or test environment**:

- Product visibility
- Add to cart and checkout flow
- Payment & shipping settings

# How to Migrate to Shopify (Step-by-Step)

Here’s what a successful migration looks like:

### Step 1: Export Your Existing Data

Download CSVs or XMLs of:

- Products
- Customers
- Orders
- Pages and posts

### Step 2: Import Into Shopify

Use:

- Shopify Admin bulk importer
- LitExtension / Cart2Cart
- Matrixify (formerly Excelify) for advanced bulk actions

### Step 3: Build Your Storefront

- Choose a Shopify theme
- Recreate core pages (Home, About, Contact)
- Upload logos, brand fonts, and colors

### Step 4: Set Up Redirects

Map old URLs → new Shopify URLs using Shopify’s native redirect manager or SEO apps like **Easy Redirects**.

### Step 5: QA & Launch

Test for:

- Broken links
- Product variants
- Mobile responsiveness
- Analytics and tracking (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, etc.)

# Shopify vs Squarespace: Which Is Better?

**Here’s the TL;DR:**

| Feature         | Shopify                             | Squarespace                |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Ecommerce       | ✅ Industry-leading                 | ⚠️ Basic, limited checkout |
| Payment Options | ✅ Shopify Payments + 100+ gateways | ✅ Stripe & PayPal only    |
| Blogging        | ✅ Strong, but basic                | ✅ Excellent               |
| SEO             | ✅ Solid for ecommerce              | ✅ Great for content-first |
| Templates       | ✅ Functional & responsive          | ✅ Beautiful & minimal     |
| Scalability     | ✅ Best for growth                  | ⚠️ Not built for scaling   |

> # Verdict:
>
> - Choose **Shopify** if you’re scaling a real product-based business.
> - Choose **Squarespace** if you’re a creative or service business selling a few products.
